01 / 08 INTRODUCTION
01 · DEFINITION

What is a Refrigerator?

❄

Definition

A refrigerator is an electrical appliance that removes heat from its inside to keep food and drinks cold.

Why do we use it?

🥛

Keep Food Fresh

It helps food stay fresh for a longer time.

🦠

Slow Microbes

Cold temperatures slow the growth of many microorganisms.

🍎

Reduce Waste

Proper cooling helps us avoid throwing away food too soon.

REMEMBER: A refrigerator slows spoilage, but it does not keep food fresh forever.
02 / 08 REFRIGERATOR DEFINITION
02 · THE PROBLEM

Why does food spoil?

Food spoilage happens when food changes and becomes less fresh or unusable.

🦠

Bacteria

Some bacteria grow in food and may cause spoilage or illness.

🍄

Molds and Yeasts

They can grow on food and change its appearance, smell, or taste.

⏳

Time and Temperature

Many foods spoil faster when left in warm conditions.

KEY IDEA: Warm conditions help many microorganisms grow faster.
03 / 08 CAUSES OF SPOILAGE
03 · THE SCIENCE

How does refrigeration work?

A refrigerator takes heat from inside and releases it into the room.

🌡️
STEP 1

Heat is Removed

The cooling system takes heat away from inside the fridge.

→
❄️
STEP 2

Food Gets Cold

The refrigerator keeps the food at a low temperature.

→
🦠
STEP 3

Growth Slows

Many microorganisms grow and reproduce more slowly.

THE ANSWER

Cold temperature = slower spoilage

Refrigeration slows spoilage. It does not kill every microorganism.

05 · EVERYDAY SCIENCE

Food safety tips

Follow these simple steps to store food properly.

01

Keep It Cold

Set the refrigerator to 4°C (40°F) or below.

02

Store Food Promptly

Refrigerate perishable food within 2 hours, or within 1 hour when it is above 32°C.

03

Cover Your Food

Use clean, covered containers to protect food from contamination.

04

Separate Raw Food

Keep raw meat away from ready-to-eat foods.

IMPORTANT: Food can be unsafe even when it looks and smells normal.
